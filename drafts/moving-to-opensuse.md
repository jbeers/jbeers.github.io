Wanted to share a cool thing I did over the weekend so you can all nerd out with me.

I've been researching openSUSE Tumbleweed for a while and was really interested in setting up a new dev environment and leverage more opensource tooling and do more in the terminal.

So far the move has been great! I had been using WSL and was doing more and more of my work in linux. I was running into really annoying issues because Windows was managing the virtual disk poorly and I was completely out of drive space. Additionally, I was having to maintain multiple developement setups between Windows and WSL.

Before I get into the weeds I'll quickly cover the high level wins:

* UGREEN NAS
  * Kopia backups
  * Wireguard
  * RustDesk
* Development setup
  * nvim
  * lazydocker
  * lazygit
  * tmux + tmuxinator
  * opencode
* Gemini

Some problems I ran into:

* openSUSE default EFI partition was way too small
* Gemini nearly messed everything up by instructing me to reboot after disabling my only graphics card driver
* Kopia configuration excluded some files I wish it had saved
